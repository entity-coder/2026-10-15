Target SGPA planner

Enter what is still ahead this semester and the SGPA you want. The planner tells you the average grade point you need in the remaining credits to get there.

SGPA = Σ(credits × grade point) ÷ Σ(credits), on the standard Indian 10-point scale. Grade scales differ between universities — check yours; your last entries are saved on this device.
